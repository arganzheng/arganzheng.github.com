--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} 随笔 / Moments — flomo-style: a card stream on the right, a sidebar on the left. `page.moments` is built by _plugins/moments.rb from the `## YYYY-MM-DD` entries of moments/YYYY-MM.md (newest first) — or, on a /moments/tag/<标签>.html page (`page.is_tag`), the entries of that tag across months; this layout never prints `{{ content }}`. Each card: a head line (timestamp · @place), the note (text with `#标签` chips, gallery, quote card, music card) and a foot with the per-entry anonymous ♡ (the section-reaction machinery of js/annotations.js, `.sec-react` placeholder, quote = `§ 2026-09-21 08:02`), a link to the comments, 分享 (the share popover of js/share.js: system sheet / 微博 / X / 微信二维码 / 复制链接) and, for the signed-in author only, 编辑 (→ /moments/post.html?edit=YYYY-MM/id) and 删除 (worker DELETE /moments) — js/moments.js wires those three. Views, the Discussion and passage reactions are keyed on the month URL (`page.comments_path`), also on the `/moments/` copy of the newest month; tag pages carry none of them (every card links back to its month). The sidebar (site.data.moments: stats, heatmap, tags, months) is static; the heatmap toggle, 随机漫步 / 每日回顾 cards and stream filters read /moments/index.json in js/moments.js.
