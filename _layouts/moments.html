Each card: a head line (timestamp · @place), the note (text with `#标签` chips, gallery, quote card, music card) and a foot with the per-entry anonymous ♡ (the section-reaction machinery of js/annotations.js, `.sec-react` placeholder, quote = `§ 2026-09-21 08:02`), a link to the comments and the entry's permalink. Views, the Discussion and passage reactions are keyed on the month URL (`page.comments_path`), also on the `/moments/` copy of the newest month; tag pages carry none of them (every card links back to its month). The sidebar (site.data.moments: stats, heatmap, tags, months) is static; the 随机漫步 / 每日回顾 cards are filled by js/moments.js from /moments/index.json.
